# V3ST

V3ST is a Nigerian-first beta for understanding naira funding, fractional tokenized stock exposure, optional income strategies, and eligible proceeds. It targets retail investing newcomers who need costs, rights, risks and access explained before an account decision.

The public landing page's action is **Explore a demo**. It needs no account and uses independent browser-local illustrative fixtures. No real funds, bank transfers, identity submissions, provider calls or local-testnet transactions occur in that demo. Every external integration in the wider application remains verified test/sandbox or simulated; Paycrest is simulated-only and actual stock-provider adapters remain disabled/unverified.

Tokenized stock exposure is not a promise of direct legal ownership, voting rights or guaranteed dividends. Valuation is not cash. Holding stock or idle cash generates no automatic income. Separately chosen strategies can earn zero and lose principal; unconverted rewards are not payable. Sale proceeds may include returned principal.

Friday processing is 08:00–23:00 Africa/Lagos for eligible confirmed proceeds, subject to conversion, consent, gross minimum and costs. It promises neither income every Friday nor bank arrival time. Demo rates/fees are illustrative beta settings, never commercial quotes or recommendations.

The approved brand is light forest-green, Geist, spacious typography and original growth artwork. The landing specification pins the captured Katana motion behavior with accessible fallbacks. Ecosystem marks identify documented providers and target networks, without asserting integration, sponsorship or endorsement.

No deployment, waitlist storage, financial API changes, authenticated dashboards or live activation is in the landing assignment.
